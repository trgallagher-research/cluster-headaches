# cluster-headaches

Resources for people with cluster headache, built by someone who has them.

## What's here

| Path | What it is |
|---|---|
| `site/index.html` | **Cluster Headache Groups** — a one-page signposting site: patient groups and trusted information in 10 non-English languages, plus clusterinfo.org / clusterfree.org for people who are undiagnosed or can't get treatment. Styled after clusterfree.org. Published as a Claude artifact: https://claude.ai/artifact/UZBFrdBw8DYxMCg5ivvCU3 |
| `research/support-groups.md` | The research behind the page: every group checked, how it was verified, and what was searched for but not found. |
| `docs/` | `app-plan.md`, `ux-research.md`, `research-dataset.md`, `open-questions.md` — planning for an attack-tracking web app (big start/stop buttons, usable mid-attack, "ended while asleep" option, opt-in public-good research dataset). |

## Status (2026-10-08)
- Signposting page: v2 live (private until shared).
- Tracking app: plan written (2026-10-08); 24 decisions pending in `docs/open-questions.md`.

The page lists groups and information. It is not medical advice, and it is not run by any of
the organisations it links to.
