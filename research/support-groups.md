Checked 2026-10-08. Origin: a friend suggested keeping a list of cluster headache groups
(starting from the Dutch Facebook group *Clusterhoofdpijn*, which Tim is a member of) and
sending people who are undiagnosed or can't get treatment to clusterinfo.org. Scope chosen:
**non-English-language** groups and information.
